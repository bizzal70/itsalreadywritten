The Pipes Of The Sewers summon 2d6 giant rats once per day. The catch is in the duration: the rats obey for d6 rounds and no longer. When the music stops, the control ends, and the swarm you called turns on whoever called it. This is not an item that rewards eagerness.

The mistake is activating early. Blow the pipes at the first sign of trouble and you burn your control window on a fight that has not developed yet. The rats scatter, do little, then go hostile while the real problem is still standing. The value sits entirely in timing. A short window spent well (blocking a corridor, pinning a fleeing target, splitting a formation) reshapes the encounter. The same window spent badly is just vermin underfoot, soon to be your vermin problem.

Treat the d6 as a budget, not a bonus. You are buying a handful of rounds of obedience and paying for them afterward regardless of how you spend them.

**At the table:** Wait until the enemies commit to a retreat, then pipe the rats into the exit tunnel to wall it off. The players spend the remaining rounds fighting a cornered foe, and when the duration lapses, the rats are between the enemy and the party, not between you and the door.
