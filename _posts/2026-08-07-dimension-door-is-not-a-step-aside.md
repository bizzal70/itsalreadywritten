Dimension Door in Shadowdark is a tier-4 spell with an instant duration and a range of self. On a successful cast it moves you, plus one willing creature, to any point you can see within 500 feet. That is the whole mechanic. No damage, no control, just relocation.

The waste case is predictable. A player burns a tier-4 slot to slide behind cover or close ten feet on a target already in reach. That is a move action dressed up as a spell. The value of Dimension Door sits in the problems ordinary movement cannot answer: the pit you cannot cross, the door that will not open before the horde arrives, the fight you need to leave while you still can. It is an exit and a bypass, not a positioning tweak.

The 500-foot limit and the "point you can see" clause are the guardrails. Line of sight keeps it honest. If the player cannot see the far ledge, they cannot land on it, and no amount of confident description changes that.

**At the table:** When a player asks to Dimension Door "just past the guard," ask what they can actually see; if the answer is the guard's back, they've spent a tier-4 slot to walk five feet.
