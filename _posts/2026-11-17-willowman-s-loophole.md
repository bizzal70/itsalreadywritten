Willowman is a tier 1 spell in Shadowdark. It works at Near range, resolves Instant, and targets a creature of level 2 or lower. The effect is a forced morale check. That is the whole spell, and the whole trick.

Morale checks normally belong to the GM's discretion and skip creatures that do not break: skeletons, zombies, the mindless and the fearless. Willowman does not ask about immunity. It names a level, it names a check, and it compels the roll. A level 2 undead that would never flee on its own is suddenly rolling against its own survival instinct, which the designers did not bother to grant it. The spell manufactures the test the creature was built to avoid.

The value is situational and specific. Against anything above level 2, it does nothing. Against the low-level undead a party actually meets early, it converts a grinding fight into a possible rout before a single additional hit lands.

**At the table:** Facing three level 2 skeletons in a corridor, cast Willowman on the lead one, roll the morale check it should have been exempt from, and if it fails, watch the formation collapse as the others lose their anchor.
