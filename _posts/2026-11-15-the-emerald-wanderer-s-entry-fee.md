The Emerald Wanderer carries 10d10 hit dice behind AC 16, which already rules out the hope that it falls over to a few lucky swings. The real tax is its immunity: mundane weapons that are neither magical nor silvered do nothing. Not reduced damage. Nothing. A fighter with a plain longsword is performing an elaborate pantomime of combat.

Meanwhile the Wanderer is working. Its chilling touch hits at +8 melee for 3d8+4, which is a serious bite into a mid-level party every round it connects. The math is lopsided on purpose. While the unarmed (effectively) members whiff against its hide, it drains hit points at a rate that punishes any plan built on grinding it down with numbers.

The fix is identity of weapon, not quantity of attackers. Magical or silvered steel bypasses the immunity; everything else should be doing something other than attacking. Flanking a creature you cannot hurt is just standing in a dangerous spot.

**At the table:** Before initiative, have each player state exactly what makes their weapon count (magic bonus or silvered), and route anyone without one into spells, aid actions, or retreat rather than wasted +8 touches to the face.
