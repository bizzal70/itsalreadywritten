The Gauntlets of Might, in Shadowdark, set your Strength to 18 (+4) while worn. Read "set," not "add." They override a lower score and do nothing for a higher one. There is no stacking, no bonus on top, no reward for already being strong.

The practical consequence: these are a floor, not a ceiling. A Strength 8 wizard who straps them on gains a full +4 swing on checks and attacks. A Strength 18 fighter gains exactly nothing but the fashion. The value of the item is inversely proportional to the arms wearing it, which is a rare and pleasing bit of design math.

Because the effect is tied to being worn, timing is the whole game. The gauntlets are useful the moment before a Strength check or a round of melee, and useless if left in the pack. Put them on when the door needs forcing, not during inventory bookkeeping three rooms earlier.

**At the table:** Gate them behind a specific obstacle (a Strength 18 door, a grapple, a collapsing portcullis), hand them to your weakest character, and watch the party economy shift as the backline suddenly volunteers to hold the line.
