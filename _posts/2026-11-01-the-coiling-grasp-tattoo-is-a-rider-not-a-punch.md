The pitch sounds like a one-shot. Spend your action, make a melee spell attack at +5 against something within 15 feet, and on a hit it takes 3d6 force damage and is grappled (escape DC 13). Players read "3d6," do the attack, and move on. That is reading half the item.

The real line is the one about the start of each of your turns. While the target stays grappled, it takes another 3d6 force damage, automatically, no attack roll required. The opening strike is the admission fee. The grapple is the subscription. A creature that fails to break DC 13 for three rounds has eaten roughly 42 points of force without you rolling to hit again, which is where the tattoo earns its attunement slot.

This is also the counterplay. The damage is tied to the grapple, not to concentration, so the pressure ends the instant the target escapes, is freed, or the grappler is incapacitated. Grappled targets can still attack, cast, and tell allies to pull them loose.

**At the table:** Have the grappled creature use its action on the Escape check (Athletics or Acrobatics vs. DC 13) instead of attacking, and the recurring 3d6 stops cold.
