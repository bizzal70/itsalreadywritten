The Dungeon Crawl Classics Wood Door Golem does not chase. It has 0' movement and waits, which is exactly why parties walk into its slam: 2d6+3 damage that also initiates a grapple. The stillness reads as harmless until someone is pinned against the frame they were trying to open.

Then the attrition starts. Non-magical weapons deal half damage, so the fighters chip away and conclude the thing is unkillable. The spellcasters fare worse, because magic resistance gives spells a flat 50% chance to simply fail. Direct casting is a coin flip you lose half the time, and nobody enjoys burning a slot to watch it fizzle against a door.

The exit is written into the creature. Fire and heat deal double damage, and that vulnerability does not care about magic resistance when the source is a mundane flame. A lit torch, a flask of oil, a brazier kicked over. The construct that shrugged off swords and spells comes apart under something a first-level character carries for free.

**At the table:** When the party stalls at half-damage swings, let a passive Intelligence check or a whiff of scorched wood cue the obvious: it's a wooden golem, and someone is holding a torch.
