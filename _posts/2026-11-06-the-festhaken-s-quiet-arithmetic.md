The bite reads as 2d6 and moves on. It does not move on. It clenches, and the clench carries 4d6 poison against a DC 15 Fort save. That is the real attack. The teeth are the delivery system, and once the Festhaken has hold of you the poison keeps arriving while the die faces climb.

Escape is where parties die. The Festhaken has a dimensional jaunt, so it repositions at will and your careful flanking line reads to it as a suggestion. It also climbs 30', which means the ceiling and the walls are valid approach vectors and the "safe" rear rank is not rear anything. Add 25% magic resistance: one spell in four simply fails before the caster rolls a save, which tends to arrive at the worst possible initiative count.

Run it as an ambush predator and it earns its stat block. The extradimensional lair gives it a bolt-hole and a choke point, and the jaunt lets it pick which isolated target to clench first.

**At the table:** Jaunt it behind the spellcaster, bite to clench, then roll the 4d6 poison and hold position so the party must break the grip before they can retrieve the body.
