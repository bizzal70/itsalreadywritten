*Issue #014 · Week of October 02, 2026*

## This Week's Verdict

The big story this week is personnel, not product: Wizards of the Coast keeps rearranging its staff while **Renegade** quietly loses **Vampire** back to **White Wolf**. Everything else is Halloween content arriving on schedule, which is to say early. Nobody errata'd anything, so we read tea leaves instead.

## Releases & Announcements

The licensing desk earned its keep. **Renegade Game Studios** renewed its **Hasbro** deal for **Transformers**, **GI Joe**, and others, but surrendered the **Vampire** license back to **White Wolf**, with an **Anne Rice** TTRPG reportedly on the way. Make of that what you will.

Over on the official side, **D&D Beyond** shipped the **Sorrow Knight**, a Shadowfell-themed Fighter subclass, as part of its latest Drop. It is subscriber-exclusive, so the "first subclass" framing comes with a turnstile. **Sterling Vermin Adventuring Co.** also landed the **Magus**, a 5.5e-compatible class, on D&D Beyond. **Wizards** continues teasing **Project Lamordia**, a modern reinterpretation of Ravenloft's Lamordia, due as an October adventure.

Third-party output was steady. **Kobold Press** posted about **Scouting Party & the Monsters of the Underworld** and has **Night Hunters** and **Night Terrors** running for 5e and **Tales of the Valiant**. **Philip Reed** released **Spooky Shadows**, 55 pages of Grimm folklore for solo Shadowdark on an iterative October funding model. **Goodman Games** and **American Mythology Productions** announced a partnership to bring **Dungeon Crawl Classics** to comics. For the collectors, **Iron Studios** has a 9.5-inch **Tarrasque** statue up for preorder at $399.99, which is roughly the per-inch going rate for regret.

Looking further out, **Wizards** confirmed **Dungeons! Illustrated Maps Of Classic Dungeons & Dragons Adventures**, a walkthrough map book landing April 27, 2027.

## Rules & Errata

No official errata crossed the desk this week. The **Sorrow Knight** and the **Magus** are new options rather than corrections, and nothing from **Shadowdark** or **DCC** amounts to a rules change. The closest thing to mechanics discourse was **Xenograg** arguing for an armor class penalty when characters skip the helmet, which is house-rule territory, not official text.

## The Community

The **changing of the guard** at Wizards drew the most ink, courtesy of **EN World** (via **Morrus**) tallying the recent hirings and firings. On the firing side, **LaTia Jacquise**, the D&D community manager, was let go. On the hiring side, **Celeste Conowitch** joined as Senior Game Developer. Two data points, one trend line, and a lot of speculation filling the gap.

Elsewhere, the **Bundle of Holding** revival of **The Merry Mushmen's** **KNOCK!** zine got loud, with **TMushmen** themselves noting the first three issues run $17.95 against a $40 print price per issue. Worth the shelf space if you tolerate old-school miscellanea.

The GM-advice crowd was in form. **Sly Flourish** preached mixed combat difficulty and, in a separate post, the gospel of "**Just Use Bears**," reskinning one reliable stat block into whatever you need. **Gnome Stew** covered taking useful player notes and the quiet art of recognizing when a **Blades in the Dark** campaign has actually ended. Counterprogramming came from the **Nat1Advice** account, which advised doing the exact same thing every combat turn, which is either satire or the most honest optimization take of the week.

**MÖRKTOBER** kicked off, with the usual **Mörk Borg** prompt threads filling the OSR timeline, including **nisjasper** confessing a title-first project called **THE CANTANKEROUS CRUSADE** and a healthy skepticism of the art-punk house style.

## On the Horizon

**Underground Oracle Publishing** fully funded **Creature Companions Expanded** on Kickstarter, a Tales of the Valiant sourcebook of companion rules for 5e-compatible games. **The Curse of the Crimson Wake** brings nautical horror to **Old-School Essentials**. On the convention front, **strangequark** is running **The Ten Bells Public House**, a **DCC** scenario, at **Dragonmeet** on November 28 at London Excel, with RPG event tickets going on sale the day after Halloween.

Bargain hunters have two weeks on **EN Publishing's** 50% off all PDFs sale, and three weeks on the **KNOCK!** bundle. **EN World's** RPG Print News flagged supplements for **Swordfish Islands' Hot Spring Island**, **Age of Vikings**, **Dolmenwood** maps, and a **Fifth Season** RPG based on the novel.
