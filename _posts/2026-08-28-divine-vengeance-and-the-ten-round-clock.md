Divine Vengeance is a tier 5 spell in Shadowdark with a range of Self and a duration of 10 rounds. The number is the point. A tier 5 spell that runs ten rounds is not a burst effect you fire and forget. It is a standing condition you impose on the fight, and it only pays out if the fight is still going and still contested when round ten arrives.

The common misplay is casting it as an opening flourish with no objective attached. Spent on nothing in particular, the duration is dead weight. Its actual leverage is in shaping the board: pressuring enemy movement, denying actions, forcing the other side to spend turns reacting instead of pressing. That work happens across rounds, not in one flashy instant, and it requires the caster to know what those rounds are supposed to accomplish before the spell goes up.

Treat the ten-round window as a plan, not a timer that happens to be long. The spell rewards foresight and punishes reflex.

**At the table:** Before allowing the cast, ask the player exactly what the next few rounds are meant to do; if the answer is "hit things," let them spend the slot and learn.
