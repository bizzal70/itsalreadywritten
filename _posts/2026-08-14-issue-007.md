*Issue #007 · Week of August 14, 2026*

## This Week's Verdict

A quiet week, which means the industry did its business in ledgers and legal filings rather than at the table. Paizo finally reappeared on **DriveThruRPG**, workers at **Rowan, Rook and Decard** organized a union, and the D&D machine kept shoveling out World of Warcraft tie-in accessories. If you were hoping for a rules bombshell, go read a good dungeon map instead.

## Releases & Announcements

The biggest logistical shift: **Paizo** has joined **DriveThruRPG**, making over 1,200 **Pathfinder** and **Starfinder** PDFs available across DTRPG and the Pathfinder/Starfinder Infinite marketplaces, per **Tribality** and **EN World**, with print options and more titles promised. This lands alongside separate reporting that Paizo may finally recover its inventory from bankrupt distributor **Diamond**, held since January 2025. Two overdue supply-chain untanglings in one week.

On the **D&D** front, **Keith Baker's** Wild West 5.5e sourcebook **Frontiers of Eberron: Quickstone** has arrived on **D&D Beyond**. **Beadle & Grimm's** is reprinting its **Curse of Strahd Legendary Edition** box set for Halloween. The **D&D World of Warcraft** book continues its slow rollout with two alternate slipcase covers (Alliance and Horde), a November DM's screen, and a line of miniatures. **Wizards** also released the **D&D: Mega D20 Decision Die**, which exists.

In the indie and OSR corners, **Seandar16** announced **Knights In White Satin** for **Shadowdark**, self-released rather than crowdfunded ("Nuts to crowdfunding"). **Night Owl Workshop** is bundling **Celtic Legends** on DriveThruRPG.

## Rules & Errata

No official errata for **D&D 5e**, **Shadowdark**, or **DCC** this week. What passes for rules content is old-school archaeology: **Random Wizard** circulated **Gary Gygax's** own 2007 house rules for OD&D, including flat stat bonuses above 14 and negative HP death thresholds scaled to level, a reminder that the game's designer never ran it as written either. The same feed ran an informal poll on initiative systems (individual, side-based, phased, simultaneous).

If you want procedure rather than nostalgia, **Sly Flourish** published "Types of Lightning Rods," on picking monsters to showcase specific character abilities, plus a short note on mixing simple combats with complex, alternate-goal encounters.

## The Community

The **Rowan, Rook and Decard** unionization is the week's item with actual weight: workers at the British publisher formed what **EN World** calls a first for the UK tabletop industry. Pair it with EN World's ongoing thread on crediting creators properly in awards and media, and there is at least one real conversation happening about who makes these books and how they get paid.

The design-argument perennial resurfaced via **Tabletop Sentinel**, where **Darrin Drader** argues modern 5E resolved the roleplayer-versus-ruleplayer question in the wrong direction, trading realism for balance, and pitches his **Daring & Dread** as the corrective. Countering the usual sneer from the other side, **thoughtpunks** posted an earnest defense of rules-light systems against their common critiques. Both are worth reading precisely because neither is a hot take.

**EN World's** "On TTRPG Kickstarters, merchandise, and markups" digs into profit margins, useful reading before you back your next slipcase edition. Elsewhere the usual honest labor continued: **Dyson Logos** posted the "Pit of Despair - The Sump" map, **The Alexandrian** reran a GURPS Space review, and **EN World's** **White Dwarf Reflections #43** revisited July 1983 and the liquidation of **Metagaming**. Some things about this hobby do not change.

## On the Horizon

Crowdfunding continues apace. **EN World's** roundup flags **Deadlands**, **Ultimate Horrors**, **Forgotten Foes**, Shadowdark sourcebooks, and more. Separately, the classic **Dark Conspiracy** returns as **Dark Conspiracy: The Unseen War**, now built on **Traveller** rules and live on **BackerKit**.

In development but not yet asking for money: **hexedpress** is building a **Dark Sun**-style setting ("Under the Anti-Sun"), now with a map and 36 backgrounds, and **RockClanProductions** resumed work on the first **Council of Gothmoor** adventure for **PF2e**. Modest, real, and refreshingly free of a countdown timer.
