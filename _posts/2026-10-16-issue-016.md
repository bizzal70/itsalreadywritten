*Issue #016 · Week of October 16, 2026*

## This Week's Verdict

The headline is labor, not lore: **Wizards of the Coast** declined to voluntarily recognize a new union, then announced it will respect a vote it would not preempt, which is roughly how these things always go. Elsewhere, **Kobold Press** remembered it used to make other people's games, and the OSR quietly proved it is larger than it looks.

## Releases & Announcements

**Kobold Press** is returning to third-party publishing with **Dark Halls**, a **Shadowdark** expansion adapting its monster stable, with **Shadowdark** creator **Kelsey Dionne** on the writing team. For a publisher that spent recent years pushing its own **Black Flag/Tales of the Valiant** line, this is a notable reversal toward the work that built it. Coverage via **Tabletop Sentinel** and **Geek Native**.

On the official **D&D** side, **Project Lamordia** landed on **D&D Beyond**, a Ravenloft adventure trilogy that recasts the Domains of Dread as modern techno-horror. **Baldman Games** released the **Legends of Greyhawk** bundle "Heralds of the Old One" on D&D Beyond, pitting parties against Iuz and the Horned Society. **Wizards of the Coast** also confirmed more **D&D/Star Wars** crossover plans for 2027, and **Dark Horse** is producing **Dark Sun** graphic novels, with the first mini arriving April 2027.

In the wider field, **Paizo** announced a **Pathfinder** action figure line with **Harbinger Studios**. **EN World**'s RPG Print News catalogs the return of **Ars Magica** alongside supplements for **Daggerheart**, **Scalemail**, **Pirate Borg**, and **Mothership**. **Barrel Rider Games** put out **White Box: Pirate Adventures**, and **Advanced Fantasy: Journeyman, Expert, Master** is now in print and PDF.

## Rules & Errata

Nothing resembling official errata crossed the desk this week for **5e**, **Shadowdark**, or **DCC**. The closest thing to rules content was **Kobold Press**'s older "dos and don'ts for 5E GMs delving into Shadowdark" guide, which resurfaced in the wake of the **Dark Halls** news, and **Sly Flourish**'s "Three Choices" post arguing for offering players three meaningful options at decision points. Useful table craft, not rulebook changes.

## The Community

The labor story dominated. **Wizards of the Coast** declined to recognize a newly formed union whose stated concerns include protections against generative AI, wages, and workers' rights to their own off-hours creations, per **EN World**. Days later, the company said it will respect the results of a union vote, likely next month. The gap between "we won't recognize you" and "we'll respect the vote" is the whole story.

The quieter but more interesting data point came from **Geek Native** citing **Groupfinder**: no single OSR title cracks the top eight non-D&D games, but combined, the OSR runs 114 active tables, edging out **Pathfinder 2E**'s 104. Evidence that the scene's fragmentation hides real volume. **Groupfinder** also reportedly crossed 10,000 users with a roughly twelve-players-per-GM bottleneck, a number no amount of enthusiasm fixes quickly.

On the lighter end, **chaosfae** and others noted **Hit Point Press** has a semi-official **D&D** class that is a Pokemon trainer in all but name, which tracks. The **Shadowdark** community kept busy, with **Tabletop Sentinel** statting the Rabbit of Caerbannog for the system and a steady stream of **Questable** quests from **busywyvern**'s "Woeful Barrows and Other Places."

A note of caution: **Tribality** this week published a run of affiliate listicles about non-GamStop casinos and betting sites. It is not TTRPG content, and readers should treat that feed accordingly.

## On the Horizon

**Underground Oracle Publishing** fully funded **Creature Companions Expanded** on Kickstarter, a **Tales of the Valiant** and 5e-compatible sourcebook for adventuring with (or as) a creature companion. **Paizo**'s modern-horror one-shot game **13 Omens** is live on Kickstarter, each session a self-contained story.

For **Shadowdark** backers, **Jerd Productions** launches two more **Dragonspine Chronicles** installments, "Terror at Dragonwing Pass" and "Under the Mountains Deep," on Kickstarter October 27. **Kobold Press**'s **Dark Halls** is pegged for 2027 and will presumably crowdfund, since, as one commenter dryly noted, system crowdfunders outsell supplement ones. On the solo front, **EN World** flagged the **OneQuests** gamebook series spanning survival horror, sci-fi, and heroic fantasy.
