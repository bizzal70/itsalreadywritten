Chant is a Tier 1 Priest spell with a range of Self and a duration of Focus. In Shadowdark, Focus means the effect persists only while you maintain it, and maintaining it blocks you from casting or focusing on anything else. The spell lets you see invisible creatures and hidden forces for as long as you hold it. That is the whole value, and it is a real one.

The common misplay is treating Chant like a passive buff you light up and forget. Cast it with nothing to look at and you have burned your Focus on an empty room. Worse, a failed cast on a d20 spellcasting check against DC 11 can lose the spell entirely until a rest, so there is a cost to firing it on a hunch. Chant pays off when you already suspect an invisible attacker, a lurking ambush, or something cloaked that you need to force into the open.

**At the table:** Before a player casts Chant, have them name what they expect to reveal. No stated target means no Focus spent, and the spell stays available for the moment it actually matters.
