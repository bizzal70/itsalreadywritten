*Issue #011 · Week of September 11, 2026*

## This Week's Verdict

The week's defining move is **Munchkin**, a game built entirely to parody roleplaying, deciding to become a roleplaying game. Elsewhere **D&D** is playtesting the Underdark for the second time in a month, which is either thoroughness or indecision depending on your mood, and **Warhammer Fantasy Roleplay 5E** slipped into PDF availability without ceremony.

## Releases & Announcements

**Lynnvander Studios** announced **Munchkin 5.5E RPG**, an officially licensed 5E-compatible game built with **Steve Jackson Games**, turning the door-kicking card game into a full campaign framework. Whether the joke sustains across an actual campaign is a question the market will answer.

**Warhammer Fantasy Roleplay 5E** is now available in PDF via preorder, with buyers getting the file immediately per **EN World**. On the licensed-nostalgia front, **Renegade Games** is reprinting the **Castle Ravenloft** board game for a February 2027 release.

Old-school shelves stayed busy. **Quest for the Unknown: The Caverns of Anamosa** brings a classic adventure to old-school systems, and **Laidback DM** continued rolling out the **Draakenslund** line for **Dragonbane**, 5E, and OSR, including the World Guide and Bestiary with multi-system stats. **EN World**'s RPG Print News flagged **The Smurfs RPG**, **Paranoia**, **Troika!**, **Voidheart Symphony**, and **Netcrawl** among the week's arrivals.

**Kobold Press** reopened its open call for community-submitted adventures.

## Rules & Errata

The main event is **D&D**'s second Underdark **Unearthed Arcana** playtest in under a month, adding three subterranean subclasses and five new species. Per **EN World**, this is a direct follow-up to the earlier Underdark Options packet, which is a lot of caverns for one autumn.

The **September 2026 D&D Beyond Drop** also landed, with a revamped playable species plus new maps, spells, monsters, and a return to a familiar locale. None of it is core-rules errata, but it is official content worth noting.

**Shadowdark** and **DCC** produced no official rules changes this week, only play reports and third-party adventures, which is the natural state of a stable ruleset.

## The Community

The **3 Wise DMs** podcast ran a **Shadowdark** one-shot and, per **Da_Gut**, treated encumbrance slots as a revelation, prompting the observation that this is how you reveal you have never left the D&D ecosystem. The episode was, apparently, still worth the time.

**Martin** live-workshopped a tidal-island fortress adventure across several posts, complete with an inverted below-waterline lighthouse, boat-stealing troglodytes, and levels that flood at high tide. It is the most functional design thread of the week. In a similar vein, **Sly Flourish** published a piece on procedural play from OD&D, BX, and Dungeon World, and **The RPG Gazette** argued that OSR exploration is fundamentally logistics, a point the tidal-fortress thread quietly proved.

On the practical GM front, the recurring advice this week converged on NPCs: **ruckerworks** noted that treating NPCs as people with ongoing lives gets players to care, and a **Ravenloft** clip made the same case about giving players an NPC worth returning for. **Gnome Stew**'s Gnomecast 245 covered pitching campaigns to players.

Industry housekeeping continued. Per **EN World**, the **Diamond** bankruptcy settlement hit a snag when **Sparkle Pop** filed an objection over inventory it still holds, with **Green Ronin** issuing a statement. The **Honor Among Thieves** sequel script is reportedly finished and hunting for a studio, and a **Ravenloft** series is in the works at Netflix.

## On the Horizon

**Sly Flourish**'s **Rise of the Lazy Game Master** continues its **Kickstarter** run, with backers already in the thousands. **ICv2** previewed its "Gifts Week 2026" coverage and an "Advent of Adventure" feature, which is the trade press reminding everyone the holiday retail cycle has begun. **Advent's Amazing Advice** posted a 2026 update to its **Stranger Things: Hunt for the Thessalhydra** prep for busy DMs.
