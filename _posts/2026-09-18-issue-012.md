*Issue #012 · Week of September 18, 2026*

## This Week's Verdict

The big noise came from **Wizards of the Coast**, where executive **Dan Ayoub** admitted the OGL episode was a "rug pull" the brand is still recovering from, which is roughly what everyone told them at the time. Meanwhile the people who actually make things kept making things: **Shadowdark** hit-point rolls, **Old-School Essentials** trips to Hell, and a German blogosphere quietly building modular dungeons. The gap between corporate contrition and community output has rarely been this legible.

## Releases & Announcements

**EN World**'s RPG Print News rounds up supplements for **Mothership** and **Dragonbane**, plus two full games hitting print: **The Mecha Hack** and **Into the Ish**. Also arriving: more dice, because there is always more dice.

Two Old-School Essentials-adjacent projects surfaced via **The Gaming Gang**. **Hellcrawl** takes OSE characters straight to Hell, and **OldMoon** promises OSR fantasy on an alien world later this year. Neither has a firm date worth trusting yet.

**Undergroundoracle Publishing** is running a Kickstarter for **Creature Companions Expanded**, a 5e-compatible sourcebook of companion and familiar rules for any class. **Arcana Unleashed**, the arcane-options sourcebook covering magical companions, factions, subclasses and backgrounds, drew a full review at **EN World**, though its **Ultimate Bundle** shipment was delayed in the US and Canada.

On the podcast front, **Pelgrane Press** put out **Ken and Robin Talk About Stuff** episode 717, and **3d6 Down The Line** re-mixed the audio for its original **Dolmenwood** campaign, with **Arden Vul** re-mixes promised to follow.

## Rules & Errata

No official errata this week, which is the honest answer rather than filler. What we have instead is **Wizards of the Coast** on process rather than mechanics: **Dan Ayoub** telling **EN World** the company is "recovering from loud public missteps" on the OGL. That is corporate reflection, not a rules change.

On the play side, the **Shadowdark** crowd continued its ongoing field study of how brutal the math actually is. A third-level priest using a d6 instead of a d4 landed at 10 hit points; a third-level wizard rolled eight. Poster **Da_Gut** worked through spell-check progression as characters level, noting the roughly 25 percent chance a first-level spell fizzles for the day, and reminded everyone that **Shadowdark** stats are straight 3d6 with a reroll only if nothing hits 14 or higher. It is the rulebook working as intended, which is to say unforgivingly.

## The Community

**Ben Milton** of **Questing Beast** launched a survey on D&D play styles, trying to measure how uniform or varied the community actually is. Because his channel skews OSR, he asked that it be shared beyond that bubble, so treat any results with the usual sampling caution.

Over in Germany, **Kritischer Fehlschlag** kicked off a collaborative "Dungeon Cluster" project, small self-contained dungeon regions with their own factions and conflicts that combine into larger adventures. Entries already include a kobold cult worshipping a long-dead purple worm and a warren of flesh-shapers. The **LoFiCon** OSR convention in Leipzig also filled the German timeline with pre-con jitters, one referee running **Times That Fry Men's Souls**, a colonial North America hexcrawl, and another running **Zombie Reign**, a Shadowdark zombie-apocalypse variant fresh from Kickstarter.

**Prismatic Wasteland** wrote up what the 2026 **Odyssey** film can teach about running games, and **Sly Flourish** offered the reliable advice to prep around what actually excites you. On the darker note, **Azukail Games**' Tabletop Mercenary asked what happens when your publisher dies, which pairs grimly with the WotC penance above.

## On the Horizon

Two crowdfunders worth watching. **Cairn Press** is preparing an expanded version of the OSR one-shot **A Sliver of Evidence**, flagged as promising by **Rollenspielblog**. **Undergroundoracle**'s **Creature Companions Expanded** is live now for the 5e companion crowd.

Further out, **OldMoon** and **Hellcrawl** are both slated for later this year, and **RockClanProductions** has the first **Council of Gothmoor** adventure, "The Old Sage," a **Pathfinder 2e** module, in proofing and headed to **DriveThruRPG** pending a proof print. **EN World**'s Podcast 414 previews Underdark playtest options for 5e, the announced **Munchkin** RPG, and the returning **Castle Ravenloft** board game. Nothing there is shipping today, so hold your wallet.
