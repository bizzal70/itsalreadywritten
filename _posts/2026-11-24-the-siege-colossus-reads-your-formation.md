The Siege Colossus in Dungeon Crawl Classics rolls 3d24 action dice per round. That is not a single big attack. That is a spread of attacks, stomps up close and ballista or trebuchet fire out to 500 feet, resolved across a dice chain most parties never budget for. The arithmetic alone decides most fights before anyone draws a sword.

The stomp deals 2d24+10 and knocks the target prone on a failed DC 20 Reflex save. Prone characters eat follow-up attacks at a discount, so one missed save compounds into several. Meanwhile the siege weapons punish the distance between you and it. A cluster of adventurers crossing open ground is the exact target this creature was built to hit.

The counter is geometry, not heroics. Break line of sight with terrain, approach from more than one bearing, and refuse to present a tidy group the ballista can bracket. Divide its action dice across multiple threats and the raw output stops landing on one body.

**At the table:** Set it at the far end of a long open hall with pillars every thirty feet, then start the party clustered at the entrance, 400 feet out, and let the first ballista bolt teach them about cover.
