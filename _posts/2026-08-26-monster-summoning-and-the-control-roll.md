Monster Summoning is a 2nd-level wizard spell in Dungeon Crawl Classics. Range 20 feet, variable duration, and everything of consequence rides on the spell check made after you draw the pentacle. That single roll sets the summoned creature's power, its obedience, and how long it sticks around. It is not a fixed conjuration. It is a negotiation, and the dice hold your end of it.

The oversight is treating it like a damage button. Casters summon, point at the enemy, and assume compliance. But a poor result does not simply produce a weaker creature. It produces one that is not yours. A failed control outcome turns the summoned thing on the caster, and now you have added a monster to your own side of the ledger. The spell rewards planning and punishes reflex.

Read correctly, the value is positional. Twenty feet is enough to drop a body into the enemy's flank, block a corridor, or split their attention before initiative settles. The creature that shifts the encounter's shape is worth more than the one that trades blows.

**At the table:** Cast Monster Summoning before the melee closes, place the creature between the party and a second wave, and keep a contingency ready for the round the control roll goes sour.
