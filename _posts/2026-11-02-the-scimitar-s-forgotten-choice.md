The Scimitar costs 8 gp, takes one gear slot, and deals 1d6 damage. None of that is remarkable. The word that matters is Finesse, which lets you roll your attack with either Strength or Dexterity. You pick. Every attack, every swing.

Most players never pick. They default to Strength because that is what a sword wants, and they leave a better Dexterity modifier sitting on the character sheet doing nothing. The attack bonus is not decoration. Against a foe with high AC, the gap between your two scores is the gap between a hit and a wasted turn. If DEX is higher, use DEX. The rule already permits it. You just have to read it.

This is not a trick or an exploit. It is the weapon working as written, which is more than can be said for how it usually gets used.

**At the table:** Before the next fight, have the Scimitar wielder compare their STR and DEX modifiers out loud, then attack with whichever is higher. If it is DEX +2 versus STR +0, that is a flat ten percent swing in their favor for zero cost.
