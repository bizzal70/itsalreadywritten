*Issue #009 · Week of August 28, 2026*

## This Week's Verdict

The old guard is back on the clock: **Dragon Kings** returns from the people who built Athas, and **Wizards of the Coast** is once again asking whether becoming a brain-eating psychic monster should be a career goal. If you like your desert dystopia with built-in psionics and your errata delivered as playtest PDFs, this was your week.

## Releases & Announcements

The headline is **Dragon Kings**, a new 5E edition led by **Timothy Brown** and a group of veteran TSR designers, billed as the spiritual successor to **Dark Sun** by Athas' original creators. It is on **BackerKit**, promises built-in psionics, and makes a point of advertising human-crafted art. **EN World** covered the announcement alongside the geeknative writeup.

On the official D&D side, **Wizards of the Coast** continues rolling out the **Arcana Unleashed** supplement and its campaign **Arcana Unleashed: Deadfall**, a level 11-20 espionage arc set in Thay against the Red Wizards and Szass Tam. **WizKids** is supporting it with a set of 47 pre-painted **Deadfall** miniatures. Also new: a **Vestige Patron Warlock** subclass surfaced in the **Arcana Unleashed** pages.

Elsewhere, **Dark Horse Books** and **Massif Press** are bringing two **LANCER** hardcovers to print, **Operation Fulcrum Dawn** and **Setting Guide Volume 1**, collecting introductory missions and setting material. **Old-School Essentials** gets an updated **Referee's Book** edition arriving in November. And for the video game crowd, **Dungeons & Dragons: Long Rest Tavern** (a cozy business sim) and the Ravenloft-set **Warlock** were both announced for 2027.

## Rules & Errata

No formal errata this week, but **Wizards of the Coast** dropped a new **Unearthed Arcana: Underdark Options** playtest featuring mind flayers and the option to become one. **Tim Eagon** noted the obvious, that turning into a brain-eating psychic monster used to be a bad ending rather than a build path. Treat it as a preview, not a rule, until it survives contact with the release schedule.

Nothing official landed for **Shadowdark** or **DCC** on the rules front. The **OSE** Referee's Book reprint is a repackaging, not a rules revision, so temper expectations.

## The Community

The retrospective mood ran deep. **ruckerworks** posted a six-way comparison review across **AD&D**, **1981 Basic (Moldvay)**, 2014 5e, 2024 5e, **Old-School Essentials Advanced Fantasy**, and **Shadowdark**, with full writeups per edition. **Gnome Stew** ran "As Your Table Ages," a reflection from a 40-year veteran, and **GoldenGrognard** resurfaced the two-hour Gary Con VIII panel with the late **Tim Kask**, TSR's first employee.

**The Rpg Gazette** published "D&D's Problem With Being Exceptional," and **Azukail Games** aired grievances in "YouTube is Screwing Small Creators Again." On the smaller-creator front, **Thought Punks** is openly crowdfunding through medical costs, and **meldar16** and **Seandar16** kept beating the drum for the **Shadowdark** adventure **Knights in White Satin** with the year's most self-aware hashtags. **Sabre Games** and Third Kingdom's weekly OSR News Roundup gave several of them a signal boost.

Usual practical advice from **Sly Flourish** (restate quest goals at session start; write fresh prep notes each session) and a campaign-ending "credits montage" tip making the rounds on Bluesky.

## On the Horizon

Besides **Dragon Kings** on BackerKit, **EN World** flagged another million-dollar TTRPG campaign in its crowdfunding roundup, plus new material from **Monte Cook Games** including Cypher and **Monsters of Rend World**. In DCC land, **The Sorcerer's Conundrum**, a DCC adventure zine from **nerdronomicon**, was in its final hours on BackerKit as a featured project. The **OSE Referee's Book** and the two **LANCER** hardcovers all land later this year, and **Gary's Appendix** Issue #10 is out for the Hells-inclined.
