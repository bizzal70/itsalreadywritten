The Swarm Spider has AC 13 and 9 hit points. Read those numbers and you will conclude it is a speed bump. Nine hit points is one good hit from most anything. The table sees fragile and treats the encounter as a formality, sending one character forward to swat it.

That is the mistake, and it is a mechanical one. The bite forces a DC 12 Constitution save. Fail, and the character is paralyzed for 1d4 rounds. A paralyzed character is not inconvenienced, they are removed from the fight and left standing among whatever else the spider brought. The creature's low hit points only save you if you kill it before it lands the bite, and a lone attacker rolling once per round is betting a bad save against a coin flip. Fragile and dangerous are not opposites here. They coexist.

The correct answer is concentration of force: multiple attacks in the first round, before anyone gives up their turn to a failed Constitution check.

**At the table:** When the spider bites, call for the DC 12 Constitution save immediately, then narrate the paralysis by having the player physically set their die aside for 1d4 rounds so the loss of turns is visible, not abstract.
