*Issue #017 · Week of October 23, 2026*

## This Week's Verdict

A quiet news week, which in this hobby means only one publisher purged its storefront and one megacorp dared its workers to hold a vote. The content mill kept grinding, the Mörktober prompts kept arriving, and somewhere a man sold **D&D-themed toilet paper** with a straight face.

## Releases & Announcements

**Chaosium** removed roughly 40 community titles from its storefront as part of a crackdown on AI-generated content, with a policy best summarized as "just don't use AI." The same move reportedly includes retroactively editing older products, which is either housekeeping or revisionism depending on which thread you read.

Elsewhere on the shelf: **Paizo** has 13 OMENS, its horror RPG, live on Kickstarter, and Pathfinder action figures are apparently a thing now. **Lynnvander Studios** is running a **Munchkin RPG** (5e-compatible) on BackerKit. **WotC** put out Misplaced Monsters: Volume Two on **D&D Beyond**, an eight-stat-block bestiary with proceeds going to Seattle Children's Hospital. EN World is also pushing a pair of **OneQuests** solo survival gamebooks and **Secrets of the Tome**, a spellcaster options book with a new class. And yes, **D&D-themed toilet paper** exists.

## Rules & Errata

Nothing official to report from the big three this week. For actual play procedure, **Sly Flourish** published "Scenes and Situations," reaffirming the eight-step prep from Return of the Lazy Dungeon Master, and separately offered the unglamorous but correct advice to run low-level campaigns to keep games fast and curb game-breaking combos. **Gnome Stew** ran "Act With Intent," a piece on getting players to describe actions the GM can actually adjudicate, born from a Blades in the Dark session. Useful, none of it binding.

## The Community

The **United Wizards of the Coast** (CWA) organizing effort is the week's real story. Per EN World's news digest, Wizards refused to voluntarily recognize the D&D and Magic worker union, forcing a vote; petitions and solidarity posts circulated across the fediverse, including German-language signal-boosting. Against this, **Jeff's Game Box** published a "let them fail" take arguing that struggling TTRPG companies, unions included, should be left to collapse. It is a position, held with confidence.

The Chaosium purge generated its own low-grade heat over whether sanitizing old products is a publisher's prerogative or something worse. And **Rascal News** ran "What Is The OSR, or How Discourse Would Be Better If You Tried Googling It," a title that does all the arguing for you. The OSR itself kept playing rather than defining: ongoing **Halls of Arden Vul** sessions, a first night of **Mythic Bastionland**, **Dolmenwood** at the table, and a full run of Mörktober prompts for **Mörk Borg**.

The usual GM-advice sediment settled as well, from making villains hateful via petty inconvenience to the perennial observation, via Reddit, that "5e gives a skeleton to flesh out, while PF2e gives a block of marble to sculpt." A fine line, repeated often enough to count as folklore.

## On the Horizon

EN World's crowdfunding roundup lists **13 Omens** (**Paizo**), **Munchkin** (**Steve Jackson Games**), **Dragon Kings**, plus work from **Monte Cook Games** and **Goodman Games**. On the zine front, **Secret Passages #4**, a retro and Warhammer zine, is on Kickstarter, and **Hexed Press** released the second issue of its rules-agnostic fantasy zine. **Pelgrane Press** dropped a new "Ken and Robin Talk About Stuff" episode titled "Our Fun Number is Gone," which is the most relatable product name of the week.
