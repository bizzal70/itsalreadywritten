In Shadowdark, Freya's Omen is a tier 4 Seer spell. It does one thing: for 1d6 rounds, failing a spell check no longer causes you to lose the spell. That is the entire contract. Read the duration again. It is short, and it is random. You do not get to pick when the clock runs out.

The common misplay is casting it early as a general insurance policy, then burning the window on nothing. By the time the fight actually turns, the omen has quietly expired and the caster is back to losing spells on a failed check like everyone else. Worse, players treat it as full immunity. It is not. It prevents spell loss on failure. It does nothing about a critical backfire, which remains as nasty as ever.

The value is entirely in timing. Spend it in front of a cast you genuinely cannot afford to lose, in a stretch where the fight will be decided inside a few rounds. Spend it on a hunch and you have spent a tier 4 slot on a rounding error.

**At the table:** Before a player casts Freya's Omen, ask which specific spell they intend to protect this round; if the answer is "just in case," let them keep the slot.
