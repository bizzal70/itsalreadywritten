Shadowdark's Wyvern reads like a flying predator that wants to bite and claw you. Tables run it that way, trade blows with the rend attacks, and treat the tail as set dressing. That is a mistake the monster will punish.

The stinger carries a poison rider: a DC 15 Constitution save, with failure adding 2d10 damage on top of the hit. That is not a scratch. Against a mid-tier party with unremarkable Constitution saves, two connecting stingers can swing a manageable skirmish into a body on the floor. The rend numbers are honest and visible. The poison is the part that quietly empties the healer's reserves.

Identity matters here. This is the Wyvern, a single-attack flyer with a lethal tail, not a dragon and not a generic "big winged thing." The threat profile is the stinger, and it should be telegraphed as such so the save actually means something.

**At the table:** Announce the stinger strike before rolling the save, call the DC 15 Constitution check out loud, and apply the 2d10 immediately on a failure so players feel the poison stacking rather than discovering it when someone drops.
