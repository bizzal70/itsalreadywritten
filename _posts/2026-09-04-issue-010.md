*Issue #010 · Week of September 04, 2026*

## This Week's Verdict
Two D&D television projects entered the week and only one walked out, which is roughly the survival rate of a first-level party in a Shadowdark dungeon. The rest of the news is a quiet AI apology and the usual OSR jam harvest, so nobody is short on free PDFs.

## Releases & Announcements
The screen news dominates. Per **EN World**, **Netflix** has a **Ravenloft** series in development and it will feature **Strahd**, while the long-rumored **Forgotten Realms** TV series is officially dead. Make of that what you will: the Mists get a camera crew, the Sword Coast gets a coffin.

On the book side, **Ahu's First Patrol** is now live on **DriveThruRPG** after a slow crawl from Kickstarter, per **marteloschwarz**. **Hit Point Press** cross-posted its ENNIE-winning 5.5E supplement **The Field Guide to Floral Dragons** onto **D&D Beyond**. **Baldman Games** put out **Legends of Greyhawk: Dreams of the Chained God**, a three-adventure digital bundle for levels 5 to 10, also on D&D Beyond. **Azukail Games** announced adventure modules for its **Sundara** setting. **Monte Cook Games** brought **Cypher** to **Roll20**. For the sci-fi crowd, **lategamer** released **We Go In**, a non-violent career and mission generator for **Traveller**. And **Old-School Essentials Quick Delve #4: Shadows and Flesh** is in stock at **Exalted Funeral**, per **The Gaming Gang**.

## Rules & Errata
No official errata from **Wizards of the Coast**, **The Arcane Library**, or **Goodman Games** crossed the wire this week. The nearest thing to a rules argument came from **Moondog** on the eternal **Vancian casting** debate, whose only real finding is that critics tend to propose replacements clunkier than the thing they hate. That is discourse, not errata.

For those actually at the table, **Sly Flourish** published a set of lazy improvised monster statistics for **Daggerheart**, aimed at building adversaries on the fly. Useful, unofficial, and honest about it.

## The Community
The AI question resurfaced. Per **EN World**, **Monte Cook Games** replaced a promotional video and issued a statement after the original was found to contain AI assets, which **Monte Cook** condemned. A studio walking something back is more useful than a studio pretending nothing happened.

Elsewhere, the OSR jam scene stayed busy. The **Mystic Woods OSR Adventure Jam** on itch.io already logged 42 entries, with **doomedzone** contributing a CC0 piece. The **Black Hack 10th Anniversary Jam** wrapped, prompting review roundups from **ng76** at Polyhedral Nonsense and a last-minute bestiary of evil clowns. The German-language **Grenzland** zine put out issue 8, themed on 2d6 games like **Traveller** and **Cepheus Engine**, free as a PDF, with a **Reptiles** issue slated for January 2027.

On the grumbling front, **gcvsa** delivered a full broadside against the current **Hasbro**-era 5e books, arguing rulebooks should function as rulebooks rather than art galleries, and naming **Whitehack**, **The Window**, and **ICRPG** as the counterexamples. **ruckerworks** reports that among six D&D and D&D-adjacent rule sets covered, **Basic D&D (Moldvay)** and **Old-School Essentials Advanced Fantasy** drew the most reader interest, which tells you where the crowd's curiosity actually sits.

If you want practical table craft, **Moondog** also posted a reframing of the "miss" as narrative parry, dodge, or glancing blow, plus **DnDFanatics** ran an interview with veteran designer **Shawn Merwin** on where D&D goes next.

## On the Horizon
The **EN World** RPG Crowdfunding roundup flagged **Skullcrusher**, **Time Without Tide**, and **Jadestorm**, among others, including new projects from **Chaosium** and **Onyx Path Publishing** and a one-dollar bargain worth a look. **Seandar16** noted a **Shadowdark** title from **meldar16** whose early-bird price closed, moving from $34.99 to $40. **ShadowWest**, pitched as Shadowdark meets the Weird West, has a video preview up. **RockClanProductions** is back working on **The Old Sage** for **Pathfinder 2e** after a bout of summer cold. And **EN World** teased its Podcast #412 livestream previewing **Arcana Unleashed**, new D&D video games, and a new world from the original **Dark Sun** creators, whose **Dragon Kings** setting has been updated to 5e.
