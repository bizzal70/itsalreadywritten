*Issue #020 · Week of November 13, 2026*

## This Week's Verdict

The big news is a denial: **Paizo** spent the week saying it did not use AI in a new **Pathfinder** book, which is the kind of headline that now arrives on schedule. Meanwhile **Wizards** continues shoveling **World of Warcraft** species into the **Forgotten Realms** at a pace that suggests someone found the Azeroth bestiary and refused to stop. A quiet week for actual rules, a loud one for sourcing anxiety.

## Releases & Announcements

The **Cosmere** line reached retail: the **Mistborn RPG**, second installment of **Brandon Sanderson**'s tabletop setting, is now in stores following a $14.7 million Kickstarter, which is a number best not dwelt on.

**EN World**'s **World of Warcraft Deep Dive** series kept grinding through the roster this week with the **Worgen**, **Vulpera**, and **Troll** entries, feeding the official **D&D** crossover. On the merchandise side, the **D&D World of Warcraft Icecrown Citadel Adventure Map Pack** got a walkthrough from **D&D Fanatics**, and the **Dungeons & Dragons** comic #1, reviewed at **EN World**, parades **Mordenkainen**, **Volo**, and **Tasha** around like an Avengers lineup.

**Secrets of the Tome** continues its drip of previews, this week introducing the **Pactweaver**, pitched as a full new class treating with spirits, plus **Professor Omega**, the Huckster Artificer NPC. Elsewhere, **Pelgrane Press** released a **13th Age 2e** premium module for **Foundry VTT**, the **Dungeon Crawler Carl RPG** showed up in **EN World**'s RPG Print News alongside supplements for **Mothership**, **Delta Green**, and **Outgunned**, and the **Shadowfinder: Game Master Guide** landed in PDF. In licensing news nobody asked for but will enjoy, **Dungeons & Kittens**, the adaptation of the French **Donjons & Chatons**, is getting a TV series.

## Rules & Errata

Thin pickings on official rules this week. **Shadowdark** saw its **Foundry VTT** system tick to version **4.1.0** via **muttley**'s Patreon, which is tooling rather than rules text, but it is the closest thing to an update on offer. **Old School Essentials** is reportedly getting new revised official rules, prompting the predictable lament from players who just got comfortable with the current printing. **Vampire: The Masquerade**'s next edition ran a fresh alpha playtest, which **EN World** reports received mixed reviews, so that one is still cooking.

No **5e** errata, no **DCC** clarifications. The rulebooks remain as they were.

## The Community

The week's throughline is AI sourcing. **Paizo** publicly denied using AI in a new **Pathfinder** book after community members flagged several pieces of art as having generated-art characteristics, a now-familiar cycle. Against that backdrop, indie creators leaned hard into "no AI" as a selling point: **Jeff Stevens Games**' **Book of the Dead & Damned** and **Jerd Productions**' **Dragonspine Chronicles** both put human-made labeling front and center.

On the eternal systems argument, **Trevor Devall**'s video on **Shadowdark** drew commentary from **Rollenspielblog**, who noted Devall is a committed trad gamer who dislikes OSR play for precisely the reasons OSR fans like it. A parallel German-language thread from **HeyeBodo** picked at a real tension in neo-trad **5e** tables: conflict over players importing feats and ancestries from new supplements against a GM's "doesn't fit the setting" veto, framed as a domain clash rather than a balance one.

Lighter fare, because the week needed it: **monkeyninja** dreamed of a lich storing its soul in a "Phil Collins Phylactery," which middle-of-the-night Noah found funnier than the Genesis timeline supports. **Sly Flourish** posted "Write For Yourself," a sensible reminder that GM notes serve the GM, not an imaginary publisher.

## On the Horizon

Crowdfunding is busy. **Book of the Dead & Damned for Shadowdark** from **Jeff Stevens Games** is live, with **skerritthegreen** flagging contributed content including the Corpse Knot. **Jerd Productions** has **Dragonspine Chronicles Volumes 2 and 3** running, **Masked Paws** from **Grimwood Games** is in its final two days and $500 short, and the **13 Omens** Kickstarter unlocked a **13 Lost Omens** crossover zine with **Pathfinder** as a stretch goal. A German-language crowdfunding for the **Old School Essentials** rulebook is also underway via **gameontabletop**.

Previews worth noting: **Chris McDowall**'s **Intergalactic Bastionland** dropped a new trailer, and **Wolden**'s free SRD went live with a one-click character generator. On the screen side, **Warhammer 40K: Dark Heresy** arrives as a video game in January 2027, adapting the **FFG** tabletop RPG. And **Level Up: Advanced 5e** is running a 50% pre-holiday sale through November 16, for those who want crunch at half price.
