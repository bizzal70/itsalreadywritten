*Issue #019 · Week of November 06, 2026*

## This Week's Verdict

The big news is a crossover supplement nobody at the table asked for and a few things management would rather you not notice. **D&D World of Warcraft** arrives, **D&D Beyond** fixes a search function that should have worked years ago, and **Wizards of the Coast** reportedly hired a union-busting law firm in the same breath it hired new designers. Read the second paragraph of every announcement.

## Releases & Announcements

The headline product is **Dungeons & Dragons: World of Warcraft**, a 5.5E supplement porting Azeroth to the tabletop. **EN World** has been running a steady drip of species deep dives, covering the **Forsaken**, **Earthen**, **Draenei**, and **Dracthyr**, plus a seven-dungeon rundown spanning Azjol-Nerub and Shadowfang Keep. Whether you wanted Horde lore in your campaign is between you and your DM.

Elsewhere, **1985 Games** put **Obojima: Tales from Yatamon**, its manga-flavored 5.5E supplement, on **D&D Beyond**. The **D&D Beyond Drops** program added the **Bariaur** and Ysgard content for subscribers, pulling from the Norse-inspired Upper Plane.

Outside the D&D orbit, **Mongoose** is reprinting the original 1977 **Traveller** box set for the game's upcoming 50th anniversary. **Paizo** continues licensing vintage horror for its rules-light **13 Omens**, adding Dracula, Frankenstein, and The Wicker Man to the already-announced Reanimator. **D20 Culture** is making a **Ben 10** TTRPG, with details promised in December, and **Candela Obscura** is getting a graphic novel series on no stated timeline.

On the personnel front, **Wizards of the Coast** hired **Lyla Fujiwara** as Senior Game Designer, who joined last month.

## Rules & Errata

Not rules exactly, but infrastructure: **D&D Beyond** rolled out a major update to its search functionality, so finding a specific rule no longer requires a successful Investigation check. That it took this long is its own quiet indictment.

For **Shadowdark**, the recurring community question was familiars, which the core books do not cover. The working answer from the floor: adapt the familiars attached to Witches in **Cursed Scrolls 1**. For **DCC**, **The Wondering Monster** published an **Apostate** class for clerics who lose their faith. Neither is official, but both are the kind of table-ready patch these systems invite. No official errata from any of the three this week.

## The Community

The item worth your attention is buried in **EN World's** October 31 news digest: **Wizards of the Coast** reportedly hired a union-busting law firm. Set that next to the Fujiwara hire and the new **WotC CEO Ayoub** noted in **EN World** Podcast #421, and you get a picture of a company staffing up and lawyering up at the same time.

**Joe Manganiello** says he hasn't given up on a **Dragonlance** TV series and hinted the long-stalled D&D documentary is closer to release. Both projects stalled under previous Hasbro leadership, so hints are all we have.

In the usual rotation, **Sly Flourish** wrapped his **Dragon Empire** prep campaign at session 87 with a Tiamat finale and published his lazy encounter benchmark for 2014 and 2024 5e. **RPGBOT.Podcast** began a "How to Play Shadowdark" series. **Gnome Stew** put out two pieces on system design, one on choosing a system for a setting with no game and one on what death mechanics reveal about a system's intentions. On the OSR side, a free compilation of over 800 netbook monsters surfaced via **deep-grounds.itch.io**, eleven years in the making and preserved from the usenet era.

## On the Horizon

The **PNW RPG Fest** is live on Kickstarter with more than a dozen OSR projects, including **Dragonspine Chronicles Vol 2&3**. **Exalted Funeral** is bringing back **The Gnomes of Levnec**. **EN World's** crowdfunding roundup flags campaigns for **Sword World**, **Spirit World**, **Rifts**, **The Seekers Guide to Dreaded Demigods**, and **Anne Rice's Immortal Universe**, among others.

For the zine-minded, **Hilander RPGs** has **Fletchwind: Out of the Cities**, system-agnostic city tools statted for **Cairn 2e**. **Cumberland Games** continues fulfilling **Through Familiar Eyes** on BackerKit. The **Old-School Essentials** collection is bundled cheap on Fanatical if your PDF pile has room.
