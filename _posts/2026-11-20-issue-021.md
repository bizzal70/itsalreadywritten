*Issue #021 · Week of November 20, 2026*

## This Week's Verdict

The hobby spent the week cleaning up after other people's crimes and other people's security vendors, which is to say it was a week about consequences. **Chaosium** severed ties with a convicted designer, **D&D Beyond** got swept up in someone else's breach, and the second-oldest RPG lost its author. The dice kept rolling anyway, as they do.

## Releases & Announcements

**Chaosium** confirmed its **Tapestry** RPG, commissioned to mark the Bayeux Tapestry's loan to **The British Museum**, launches at the museum's "Reimagining the Bayeux Tapestry" event on December 4th. Separately and far less celebratory, Chaosium halted production of its **Call of Cthulhu** board game and canceled **Samuel Bailey's** contract on **Old One Origins** after Bailey was convicted of seven crimes, including child rape. Chaosium states it did not know of the charges until last week.

On the 5E front, **Underground Oracle Publishing** opened late pledges for **Creature Companions Expanded**, a **Tales of the Valiant** compatible sourcebook. **Secrets of the Tome**, the 5E book carrying the full new **Pactweaver** class alongside the Moon Priest, Reverent, and Spirits options, went live on Kickstarter. **Paizo** announced **Pathfinder** lore books focused on **Arcadia** for fall 2027. **Mongoose Publishing** posted its annual "State of the Mongoose" review of the company's operations.

For the actual-play watchers: **Chris Perkins** returns to the chair with **Critical Role's** "Reign of the Weredragon," a **Daggerheart** series launching January 2027 and run as an open-book GM workshop with live Discord audience polls. **The Mighty Nein** season two lands next March.

## Rules & Errata

No official errata crossed the desk this week for **5e**, **Shadowdark**, or **DCC**. What we have instead is **EN World's** ongoing **World of Warcraft** crossover coverage, which reads like a changelog: new spells for the **Death Knight** (ten), **Demon Hunter** (thirteen), and **Druid** (nine, plus two Wild Shape forms), new Elf heritages, and the decision to build the **Shaman** on the barbarian chassis rather than the druid, a choice the designers admit raised eyebrows.

The real rules thinking happened in the **OSR** corners, where it usually does. One poster asked whether **Charm Person** is broken in 1974 **OD&D** after a first-level magic user charmed both a 7th and a 5th level caster from random encounters, which is less a bug than a feature of the three little brown books. Another floated letting **Remove Curse** roll against the curser on the Turning Undead table. House rules, not canon, but better reading than most patch notes.

## The Community

The week's hard news was criminal. Beyond the Bailey conviction, former **Dragon Con** co-founder **Edward Kramer** was sentenced to 40 years following three counts of child molestation in Gwinnett County. The convention cut ties in 2013.

**Ken St. Andre**, creator of **Tunnels & Trolls**, the second TTRPG ever published, died at 79. If you want to honor the man, **nisjasper** happens to be mid-series on a sardonic but affectionate walk through T&T character creation, which is about the right tone for the game.

Elsewhere, the usual skirmishes. One **dice.camp** poster declared the term "OSR" dead and meaningless, citing a 2022 blog post titled "The OSR Should Die," which is itself a very OSR way to spend an afternoon. On the craft side, **Domainofmanythings** broke down a four-step room-key method cribbed from **Necrotic Gnome's** new **Beneath the Radiant Dome**, 83 keyed areas in 56 A5 pages, terse keys being a skill worth stealing. **Sly Flourish** published "Start With a Map" for anyone staring at a blank page, and **Gnome Stew** confessed to the pain of leaving an 18-month **Blades in the Dark** game for **Xcrawl Classics**.

## On the Horizon

**EN World's** crowdfunding roundup flagged **Twilight Sword**, **The Dark Beneath**, **Creed's Codex**, and a game about class struggle in Victorian London, the usual spread of monster books, campaigns, and 5e/5.5e class options. **Gooey Cube's** **Alphinius Goo** talked up two boxed sets tied to **Luke Gygax** and **The Temple of Inquinatious Evil**. **themightyjerd** entered the final days of a **Shadowdark** Kickstarter bundling two OSR one-shots with 11 new monsters across PDF, print, and Foundry. For the younger table, **Rosebound Press** has **Tiny Torches Vol. 1: The Secrets of Bramblewood**, a Shadowdark intro aimed at ages 7 to 10.

One housekeeping note worth your calendar: **D&D Beyond** was among hundreds of sites hit in the **Optimizely** third-party breach on Monday. The platform claims no payment information was accessed. Change your password anyway.
