*Issue #013 · Week of September 25, 2026*

## This Week's Verdict

The crossover machine keeps grinding: **World of Warcraft** subclasses that quietly rewrite class features, a **Dragonlance** world book that arrived by way of an actor's social feed, and a redesigned **Bahamut** whose stats live in a comic book. Meanwhile a British convention finally said the quiet part out loud about AI. A normal week, then, which is to say a strange one.

## Releases & Announcements

The headline item remains **Dungeons & Dragons**' upcoming **World of Warcraft** book, due November, which **Wizards of the Coast** has begun previewing. Per **EN World**, several of the new subclasses do more than bolt on abilities; they swap out classic class features for new ones, which is a bolder design choice than the usual reskin. The same publisher's redesigned **Bahamut** will have his stats printed in an upcoming D&D comic, because that is apparently where god statblocks live now.

On the setting front, **Weis**, **Hickman**, and **Joe Manganiello** confirmed a **Dragonlance** world book for the **D&D Icons** line, with an update suggesting the timeline sits alongside the original **Dragonlance Chronicles**. **Games Workshop** is producing a **Necromunda** TTRPG as a companion to the skirmish game, and **Paranoia** has a new adventure pointing its satire at social media influencers, which is either brave or redundant.

Pricing news for the ledger-keepers: **Paizo** raises **Pathfinder** and **Starfinder** prices on October 1st. **Modiphius** is running a warehouse clearance with hardcovers of **Dune** and **Star Trek Adventures** at rock-bottom numbers, so the two events roughly cancel out if you shop carefully.

## Rules & Errata

Thin week for official errata, so we take our rules content where we find it. **Sly Flourish** published an initiative variant, **Elective Action Order**, adding another option to the pile alongside the standard **D&D** d20-plus-dex method and **Shadowdark**'s approach. On the table, a solo **Shadowdark** player noted the system's sensible design of applying the Constitution modifier to hit points only at first level, which strips out the stat's usual outsized importance.

The community also supplied a genuine 5e rules question worth flagging: whether a character dropped to negative hit points well past their maximum (the example given was -19) still rolls death saves. The **2014** rules answer, for those keeping score, is instant death when negative damage exceeds your hit point maximum, no saves involved. A useful thing to know before your BBEG connects.

## The Community

The sharpest real news is **UK Games Expo** finally banning AI from the convention, a ban that extends to merchandise, clothing, and art. That decision landed the same week **Free League** kept insisting it wanted human illustration, and it stands in tidy contrast to the WoW crossover economy above.

Elsewhere, the usual and the useful. **Sly Flourish** warned against chaining detrimental effects like stun-locking or mass-sleeping your monsters. A **dice.camp** poster asked the perennial question of whether "5e is best" people have played anything else, which is a fight as old as the hobby. **Cubicle 7**'s long-quiet **C7d20** 5e variant got a public "does anyone remember this" prod, the answer to which appears to be a shrug. And **Pelgrane Press** dropped a new **Ken and Robin Talk About Stuff** episode titled "Mad at Gary," which is a mood as much as a topic.

## On the Horizon

Crowdfunding is where the money is this week. **Free League**'s **The One Ring** Bestiary and Gondor campaign is nearing two million, offering both updated original rules and 5e support plus the obligatory cloth maps. **The Griffon's Saddlebag** books 3 and 4 cleared $900k with a week left and a shot at seven figures. **Underground Oracle Publishing** launched **Creature Companions Expanded**, a **Tales of the Valiant** sourcebook for adventuring with (or as) a creature companion. **Pulp Hummock Press** is funding **The Overlords of Steel** for **OSE**, and **EN World**'s crowdfunding roundup catalogs the rest, from cooking monsters to Jane Austen.

Conventions worth marking: **Dragonmeet** in London is expanding toward three days within two years, while **AireCon** in Harrogate is running its stated final edition next March. **Lucca Comics & Games** turns 60 with a heavy RPG lineup, including the first translated **Daggerheart** expansion from **Critical Role** and more Manganiello-led classic D&D. And an **Underdark** sourcebook appears to be brewing behind recent **Unearthed Arcana** playtests, which would give Bahamut somewhere darker to be.
