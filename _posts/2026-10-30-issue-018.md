*Issue #018 · Week of October 30, 2026*

## This Week's Verdict

The week's throughline is labor, not lore: **Wizards of the Coast** installed a new boss and a union-avoidance law firm in the same breath, which tells you where the real campaign is being run. Meanwhile the **D&D World of Warcraft** crossover limped toward release behind shipping delays, and a thirty-year-old Japanese RPG quietly out-earned nearly everyone. Read the filings, not the fan hype.

## Releases & Announcements

The loudest product news remains **D&D World of Warcraft**, with all 16 species now revealed and the designer giving **EN World** a rundown of species, classes, and how classes that did not map cleanly got handled, including the new **Death Knight**. Covered in detail by **DnDFanatics**.

Licensing deals multiplied. **Paizo** licensed **Re-Animator** for **13 Omens**, billed as the first in a planned line. **Clive Barker's Hellraiser** is becoming a TTRPG designed by **Francesco Nepitello**, co-creator of **The One Ring**. **The Army Painter** announced an official **Dungeons & Dragons** paint line for 2027. **Renegade** showed its **Interview with the Vampire** starter set at **Gamehole Con**, and **Tiny Furniture** released 3D files for a **Matt Mercer** miniature coyly named "Great Dungeon Master."

On the digital side, **D&D Beyond** finally replaced the legacy search function that predated the current site, and the **Embers** character and campaign app landed on Android. **EN World's** RPG Print News flagged supplements for **The One Ring**, **Coriolis**, **Ruins of Symbaroum**, **Liminal Horror**, and **RuneQuest**. **Traveller** got **The Fall of Lanth**. Less dignified: **Dungeon & Dookie**, licensed toilet paper, per the **EN World** news digest.

The **Necromunda** RPG, for the record, is not happening after all.

## Rules & Errata

Nothing resembling official errata crossed the desk this week for **D&D 5e**, **Shadowdark**, or **DCC**. What passes for rules content was analysis and advice.

**EN World** dug into the magic items in **Secrets of the Tome**, which also adds subclasses, spells, and a new full-caster class called the **pactweaver**. The same outlet's **RPG Evolution** column applied a technology-tier framework to D&D's armor list and found the historical compression even steeper than the gear list. On the table-facing side, **Sly Flourish** published "Manage Rests," arguing that rest frequency quietly governs pacing and difficulty more than most GMs admit, and offered a terse tip that investigations need clear goals and clear situations. Useful, unglamorous, correct.

## The Community

The story worth knowing: **EN World** reports **Wizards of the Coast** retained **Fisher Phillips**, a well-known anti-union firm, ahead of a staff vote scheduled for **November 10th**. In the same stretch, **Dan Ayoub**, previously head of the D&D division, was named head of WotC. Draw your own organizational chart.

AI policy remained a sore point. The **EN World** news digest notes **Chaosium** pulled around 40 community titles for violating its AI policy, a move echoed in the **Podcast #420** rundown framing it as "Chaosium vs. AI." Smaller creators leaned into the contrast, with **Jerd Productions** stamping its **Dragonspine Chronicles** Kickstarter "No AI used, all 100% human made."

The OSR crowd ran a quieter, more useful thread. **HeyeBodo** opened a multi-part discussion on sustaining campaign play at open tables, landing on megadungeon and West Marches structures, with **mdhughes** sharing the memorable case of a player's gnome "gone native" with kobolds between sessions. **Martin** argued, plausibly, that most D&D tables are closer to renaissance than medieval given printed books and a middle class. And a recurring "first five RPGs" meme surfaced buried gems, including one player's survival of **Fantasy Wargaming**.

On the absurdist margin: **The Price Is Right** aired a **Dungeons & Dragons** episode on October 30th. No, seriously.

## On the Horizon

The headline number belongs to **Mugen Gaming**, whose English localization of **Sword World RPG** raised two million dollars in 24 hours, per **Tribality**. Japan's oldest TTRPG arriving in English on that scale is the quiet story of the season.

**Critical Role** visits Middle Earth in November with "The Rings of Power: A Middle Earth One-Shot," run by **Liam O'Brien**. Crowdfunding elsewhere includes **Jerd Productions'** **Dragonspine Chronicles Volumes 2 & 3** for **Shadowdark**, the **Munchkin 5.5e RPG** from **Lynnvander Studios** on BackerKit, and a German edition of the telenovela game **Pasión de las Pasiones** from **Plotbunny Games**.

Comics readers can watch for **Red Sonja / Barbarella** #1, previewed via **ICv2**. On the delay watch: those **D&D World of Warcraft** books may miss early local-store access in North America over shipping, though **EN World** still expects arrival by **November 17th**.
