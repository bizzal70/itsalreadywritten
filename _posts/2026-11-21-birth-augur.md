At character creation in Dungeon Crawl Classics, you roll a d30 on the Birth Augur table. The result assigns your Luck modifier to a specific category of roll for the life of the character. "Born under the loom" ties it to spell checks. "The broken star" ties it to corruption rolls. "Lucky sign" applies it to melee attack rolls. The modifier itself comes from your Luck score (the standard ability modifier, so a Luck of 16 grants +2, a Luck of 6 grants -1). That modifier then rides on the augured roll type every single time, automatically, forever.

The common error is treating this as the same resource as spending Luck points for a temporary boost. It is not. The Birth Augur bonus is passive and permanent and costs nothing. Burning Luck points to improve an unrelated d20 roll is a separate action, and doing so lowers your Luck score, which in turn shrinks the permanent Birth Augur modifier. Players who forget this quietly erode their own standing bonus.

**At the table:** A warrior with "Pathfinder" (+Luck to missile fire) and Luck 14 adds +1 to every ranged attack for free. Tell the player before they spend Luck down to 12 and lose it.
