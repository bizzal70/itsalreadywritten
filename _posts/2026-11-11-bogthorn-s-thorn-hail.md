Everyone fixates on the stab. The stab is the distraction. The Bogthorn's thorn hail fires at near range with a +2 attack and deals a trivial 1d4 damage, which is exactly why parties shrug it off. The damage was never the point. The attached DC 12 Constitution save is.

Fail that save and you are paralyzed for 1d4 rounds. Against a creature that can keep lobbing thorns, one failed save tends to produce the next one, because paralyzed targets do not move, do not act, and do not leave near range. That is the whole engine: a cheap attack that converts position into a lockdown, then isolates the locked-down character while the rest of the party scrambles.

Treating the Bogthorn as a melee threat is the error. Its melee is the weaker option. The thorn hail is the win condition, and it only functions if your people stand still inside its near range long enough to roll Constitution repeatedly.

**At the table:** Open with the thorn hail against the party's lowest-CON member, then keep firing at the same target each round to extend the paralysis and strand them while the group decides whether to close or retreat.
