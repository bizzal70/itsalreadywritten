The Cloak of Elvenkind does two things in Shadowdark, and players tend to only remember the better one. First, it makes hiding checks easy, a flat DC 9 whenever you want to disappear from view. That part is reliable and quiet, which is the whole point.

The second function is invisibility, and this is where expectations outrun the text. The effect lasts five rounds. Not an encounter, not "until you leave the room," five rounds. It also ends the instant you attack or cast a spell. So the fantasy of winking out mid-fight, stabbing someone, and vanishing again does not exist. The moment you act, you are visible, and the clock was already running.

Used correctly, the cloak is a tool for leaving, not for fighting. Five rounds of unseen movement covers a retreat down a corridor, a slip past a sentry, or a withdrawal from a losing room. Activate it when combat is unlikely and detection is the only threat. Burn it at initiative and you have wasted it.

**At the table:** When a player pops the cloak to "assassinate" the guard captain, remind them the invisibility ends the second the blade connects, then start counting rounds out loud.
