In D&D 5e, Pass without Trace is a 2nd-level abjuration, concentration, up to 1 hour. Every creature of your choice within 30 feet of you gains a +10 bonus to Dexterity (Stealth) checks and cannot be tracked except by magical means. Read the bonus again. It is not advantage. It is not a d4. It is a flat +10, added to the d20 before anyone argues about the roll.

The practical effect is that typical passive Perception, usually sitting between 10 and 15, stops functioning as a wall. A party rogue with +6 Stealth now rolls at +16. Against a DC 15 guard, the floor result on the die (a natural 1, total 17) still clears the mark. The whole group, heavy armor and all, moves like the rogue does.

The failure mode is timing. Cast it as a combat opener and the first stray hit forces a Constitution saving throw (DC 10 or half the damage, whichever is higher) to hold concentration. Cast it while still unseen, and nothing threatens the spell.

**At the table:** Cast it one round before the party enters the corridor, then approach the sentries. A +16 Stealth against passive 13 means they walk past without a check being called.
