The Rust Spider (DCC) does not need to touch your gear to eat it. Its rust glob is a ranged attack, effective out to 30 feet, and it corrodes metal on contact. Plate becomes scrap. A sword becomes a stick. The party's investment in equipment evaporates in a single die roll, and there is no saving throw that hands the armor back afterward.

The tactical wrinkle is the movement. At 40 feet of ground speed with a 20 foot climb, the Rust Spider treats walls and ceilings as its preferred firing position. It ascends beyond the reach of melee, spits down, and repositions before the party can close. A fight against a Rust Spider on open ground is a different problem than one in a cavern with vertical surfaces, and the spider will choose the cavern.

The counter is unglamorous. Non-metal weapons (clubs, staves, stone, spells) keep working after the globs land. Ranged answers that do not care about corrosion end the encounter before the armor bill comes due.

**At the table:** Place the Rust Spider on a 25 foot ledge above a narrow passage so it can glob the front line, then climb out of reach before anyone reaches a wall.
