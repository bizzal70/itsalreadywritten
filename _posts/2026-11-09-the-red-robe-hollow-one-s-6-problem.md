The Red Robe Hollow One carries only 2d8 hit dice and AC 12, numbers that invite a judge to treat it as a speed bump. The spell check corrects that impression. At +6 for 1st-level spells, it clears most spell DCs comfortably and rolls its mercurial manifestations more often than the stat block suggests a creature this fragile should.

Two spells do the work. Flaming Hands on a d24 action die turns a clustered party into an area-damage problem before anyone closes. Ward Portal on a d16 action die shapes the fight by sealing exits and dictating where bodies move. Add its capacity to split and spatial shift, and a single Hollow One becomes several angles of pressure that reposition faster than melee can chase. The frailty is real, but only if you reach it.

The counterplay is identity-based, not damage-based. A +6 spell check still answers to anything that forces a check at disadvantage or interrupts the caster's action. Break the casting and the 2d8 reasserts itself.

**At the table:** Collapse the gap and pin it in melee to exploit AC 12, then land any disruption on the spell check before it drops Flaming Hands or shifts out of reach.
