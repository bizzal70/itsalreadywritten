The Winter Wolf, from Shadowdark, carries Frost Breath: a near-sized cube of cold, DC 15 DEX save or 3d8 damage. That is up to 24 points to everyone caught inside, halved on a success, which is still a serious bite out of a low-level sheet.

The trap is the recharge. Most breath weapons stay down long enough for players to forget they exist. This one returns in 1d4 rounds. Plan around the usual long cooldown and you will eat the cone again before anyone has moved. Cluster three or four characters in that cube and a single roll can gut the fight in your players' favor or yours, depending on who read the stat block.

Area control is the whole point of the creature. Spread out and the cube catches one body. Stack up to focus fire and you hand the wolf its best outcome. The math does not care about intentions, only standing positions when the breath recharges.

**At the table:** Open the encounter on a frozen ledge or narrow bridge that tempts the party to bunch, then recharge Frost Breath on the first 1d4 you roll and center the cube on the tightest cluster.
