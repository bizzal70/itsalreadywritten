The Draconic Bloodline subclass (Player's Handbook, Sorcerer) is not a prepared caster. A Sorcerer knows a small fixed list from the Spells Known column (fifteen spells total at level 20) and casts from the standard Sorcerer slot table: two first level slots at level 1, climbing to a single sixth-level slot only at level 11. Flexible Casting lets you convert slots to Sorcery Points and back, but that pool is capped at your Sorcerer level, so it moves power around rather than creating more of it.

The practical breakpoint is spending discipline. Burning a third level slot on a Fireball (8d6, Dexterity save, DC equal to 8 plus proficiency plus Charisma) against a pack of goblins means that slot is gone when the actual threat arrives. A Draconic Sorcerer cannot re-prepare to cover the gap the way a Wizard can after a long rest. The slots you do not spend are the only reserve you have.

**At the table:** Before casting, ask whether the encounter is worth a leveled slot or whether a cantrip (Fire Bolt, 1d10 scaling to 4d10) closes it; save the 3rd level slots for the fight the DM clearly built.
