*Issue #008 · Week of August 21, 2026*

## This Week's Verdict

Wizards is playtesting a subclass path that lets a player character become a mind flayer, which is either bold design or a HR complaint waiting to happen. Elsewhere the Dark Sun discourse warms up two years early, and the OSR corner keeps proving that fewer stats produce more game. A quiet week where the loudest thing was a poem in a Shadowdark dungeon.

## Releases & Announcements

The main indie event was **Knights in White Satin**, a Shadowdark dungeon from **Wind-Up Vampire** (**Seandar16** and **meldar16**), out this week in print through their site and as PDF on **itch.io** and the new **RPG Trader** storefront. It runs 86 pages spiral-bound with a full-color art zine, and there is an early-bird discount plus a full-catalog bundle for $13.12. It will also be sold in person at the Peabody Heights events in Baltimore run by **Justin** of **Severed Books**.

On the licensed 5e side, **EN World** flagged **Alpha Star** issue 3, the quarterly sci-fi magazine compatible with core **D&D 5E** and **Level Up: Advanced 5E**, with starlane scum, jungle beasts, a murder mystery, and a new heritage. **Kobold Press** shipped the **Northlands Worldbook** and **Northlands Sagas** for **5e** and **Tales of the Valiant**. **D&D Beyond**'s August 2026 drop landed with sewer-themed magic items, maps, and monsters, and new art surfaced for the upcoming **D&D: World of Warcraft** sourcebook.

**EN World**'s RPG Print News rounded up **Scalemail**, the **Invincible RPG**, and supplements for **Castles & Crusades**, **Troika!**, **Blades in the Dark**, and **Cohors Cthulhu**. On the OSR shelf, **Tomb of Tog** arrived for **Old-School Essentials** and **Rock & Roar!** brought classic rock to old-school fantasy.

## Rules & Errata

The headline is a **Dungeons & Dragons** Unearthed Arcana playtest of Underdark options: three new subclasses, a volatile new form of magic, and a player-focused transformation into a mind flayer. Reserve judgment until the survey data comes back, but "become the thing your party is supposed to fear" is a design promise WotC will have to actually deliver on the sheet.

For the **5.5e Dark Sun** setting expansion billed as **Dark Sun: Edge of Oblivion** (dated 2027), the open question is which of Athas's original edges get sanded off under the "mature" label. That is a preview conversation, not errata, so no rules text exists to argue over yet.

No official Shadowdark or DCC errata this week. **Knights in White Satin** does the more interesting thing, using cloth, moonlight, and poetry as obstacles, which is homebrew content rather than a rules change.

## The Community

The **Dark Sun** thread on **EN World** is the predictable flashpoint, asking whether the setting's slavery and other harsh elements survive a modern release. Worth watching, mostly for how WotC frames "mature."

The OSR blogs kept the good stuff coming. **The RPG Gazette** argued in "Less is More" that OSR games feel bigger than their character sheets, and **Luke Gearing** posted on wizards. **The Alexandrian** reprinted an old RPGNet review of **Penumbra Backdrops**, and a post at barenuckelavee on "no one can take your game" made the rounds. **Martin** turned the level-0 generator **Not Long For This World** into a Perchance tool, which is exactly the kind of low-stakes tinkering the scene runs on.

A small but honest AI-adjacent dust-up: **mhd** complained that a well-known German TTRPG map had been run through an AI route-planner and relabeled, then got lectured that upscaling would have been worse. Maps as art, not just diagrams, remains a hill worth defending. Someone also asked the room for verdicts on **Daggerheart**, which suggests the early-adopter dust has settled enough for the curious to wander in.

## On the Horizon

**XCrawl Classics 2E** got a live first look on **The Gaming Gang Dispatch**, which should interest the DCC-adjacent crowd. **Gareth Ryder-Hanrahan**'s **Merryshire Detective Club**, a cozy fantasy murder-mystery **GUMSHOE** game, is in the pipeline via **Pelgrane Press**-adjacent coverage. **EN World** rounded up Odyssey-themed crowdfunders and the usual mix of finished, live, and imminent campaigns.

For the DIY-minded, the **Mystic Wood OSR** adventure jam runs August 21 through September 4, fifteen days to build one adventure. And the **Cosmere Stormlight Starter Set** is now in hands, with unboxings incoming for anyone tracking the **Brandon Sanderson** license.
