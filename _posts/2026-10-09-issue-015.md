*Issue #015 · Week of October 09, 2026*

## This Week's Verdict

The week's big numbers are 25 and 13: **Goodman Games** hits its silver anniversary while **D&D x World of Warcraft** arrives with thirteen classes, which is roughly twelve more than anyone asked for. Everything else is a steady drip of zines, play reports, and the usual October obligation to produce art prompts on a schedule.

## Releases & Announcements

**Goodman Games** marks 25 years with two hardcover history-and-art books and a custom 25-sided die, per **Geek Native**. Fitting for the people who still sell **DCC** adventures with modules printed inside the cover.

The **D&D x World of Warcraft** supplement from **Wizards of the Coast** has had all 13 of its classes revealed, with **Jason Volk** of **D&D Fanatics** breaking down the MMO-inspired options. **Sirius Dice** is attaching itself to the crossover with Alliance- and Horde-themed Adventure Dice, and German retailers are already fielding the limited-edition-slipcase-versus-alternate-cover question, which tells you where this is headed commercially.

Elsewhere, **Catalyst Game Labs** gets **Anarchy 2.0** for **Shadowrun** (via **ICv2**). **Underground Oracle Publishing** released **Cypher Shorts: Dark Supers Shorts Vol. 2**. On the self-published front, **antrododm** put two zines for the Shadowdark hack **Shadows over Sanctuary** on itch, and **doomedzone** released **Doomed to Die** issue 3, free and CC0, covering an order of oathbreaking knights.

## Rules & Errata

The **October 2026 D&D Beyond Drop** has landed with a new subclass plus monsters, maps, and feats themed to the Shadowfell, per **D&D Fanatics**, which also flags a new **Magus** class and **Sorrow Knight** fighter subclass circulating this cycle. **Wizards** has not published anything here that constitutes formal errata, only content.

No official **Shadowdark** or **DCC** rules changes this week. **Inkwell Ideas** is running a Shadowdark post-a-day for October, with a Luck Token entry that qualifies as a house rule at best.

## The Community

The perennial "is 5e too complex" argument surfaced again when **fuzzyface** asked whether anyone wants to return to original D&D, complaining about slow play and too many skills. Meanwhile **ruckerworks** published a genuinely useful analysis placing **G1-3: Against the Giants**, **T1: The Village of Hommlet**, and **B2: The Keep on the Borderlands** on a railroad-to-sandbox spectrum, with Hommlet sitting in the middle.

**strangequark** won the **2026 Road Crew Encounter Design Contest** from **Goodman Games** with "The Bodies of Ballyroney Bog," which should see print somewhere. **MÖRKTOBER** continues to fill timelines with daily **Mörk Borg** art prompts, and **spiceomancy** collected a group-made castle where each room was submitted by a different blogger with zero coordination, which is either delightful or a cautionary tale about dungeon design.

For the toolmakers: **embersLucas** deployed an Android app for Embers tools to the Play Store, invite-only for testing, and added spellcasting DC modifiers for 5e and 5.5e.

## On the Horizon

**Underground Oracle Publishing**'s **Creature Companions Expanded**, a **Tales of the Valiant**-compatible sourcebook for adventuring with (or as) a creature companion, is live and funded on **Kickstarter**. **The Merry Mushmen** have put the first three issues of **KNOCK!** on **Bundle of Holding**, which is the cheapest the magazine will ever be. **RockClanProductions** is heading to **Hal-Con** with hardcover **ANDERS** core books and softcover modules for **ANDERS** and **Valtetine**.
