In DCC, Whirling Doom conjures a stationary wall of spinning blades, duration one round per caster level, placeable anywhere within 50 feet per caster level. The reach is the point most tables miss. You are not forced to drop it on your own toes. You can set it down the corridor and reshape the fight before anyone crosses it.

The damage line gets all the attention and deserves the least of it. The barrier is a movement problem for the enemy. It blocks advance, seals retreat, and grants cover to anything standing behind it. A caster who treats the spell as a mobile fence rather than a meat grinder changes what the opposition is allowed to do that turn, which is worth more than a handful of hit points.

Position matters because the thing does not move once cast. Read the room before you commit. A barrier placed to cut a melee pack off from your back line is doing more work than one placed to tickle a creature already adjacent to the fighter.

**At the table:** With foes 50 feet out, cast it across the chokepoint between you and them, stranding the advance behind spinning blades for one round per level while your party resets.
