Fire Resistance is a 2nd-level wizard spell in Dungeon Crawl Classics, and its value is entirely a function of when you cast it. The spell check determines everything. A result in the lower bands grants resistance to heat and normal fire for a single turn. Higher checks push the duration longer, widen the radius from caster-only to the whole party, and escalate the protection from mere heat tolerance up to negating dragon breath and magical flame. The dice decide the tier, so the same spell is a cantrip or a battlefield wall depending on the roll.

The common mistake is treating it as a reaction. A wizard who casts after the fire elemental has already acted spends an action and a check result on damage that already landed. Fire Resistance is a preemptive buff. Cast it when you know the hazard is coming, a lava chamber, a red dragon's lair, a corridor lined with flame jets, and the one-turn minimum covers the approach and the opening exchange.

Judges should also remember the check is a check: a low roll still consumes the casting and may risk the usual DCC spell loss on a natural 1.

**At the table:** Before the party opens the door to the dragon's hall, have the wizard roll Fire Resistance and announce the tier, so the group knows whether they got one turn of personal heat tolerance or a party-wide shield against the breath weapon.
