The Grick's Multiattack is one Beak and one Tentacles strike. The Tentacles deal slashing damage; the Beak follows for piercing. Neither hit is remarkable on its own. With 54 hit points and AC 14 from natural armor, it survives the opening round, and that survival is the whole point. The creature is built to keep acting.

What the stat block actually rewards is its 30-foot climb speed. A Grick on a cave wall or ceiling is a moving target that most melee parties cannot reach without a Dash, a reach weapon, or a ranged option. It strikes, climbs out of range, and strikes again next turn. Groups that treat it as a hit-point sponge stand in the open trading blows and lose tempo while it dictates where the fight happens.

The tactical error is engaging it on flat ground. In the stone passages where Gricks live, the terrain is the stat block. Deny the vertical surface and you strip the creature of its only real advantage, leaving a middling brute with two attacks.

**At the table:** Run the Grick on a ceiling or a shaft wall, strike with Tentacles, then climb 30 feet up out of melee reach before the party's turn.
